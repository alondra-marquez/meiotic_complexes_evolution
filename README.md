# Comparative Evolution of Meiotic Complexes in Plants and Fungi

This repository contains the data organization, code, results, and documentation for a comparative molecular evolutionary study of genes associated with meiotic complexes in plants and fungi.

## Overview 

This project investigates patterns of conservation, divergence, and molecular evolution among genes involved in meiotic processes across plant and fungal species.

The study includes protein sequence curation, gene-family phylogenies, coding-sequence validation, protein and codon-level evolutionary analyses, and tests for evidence of selection.

The initial dataset contains approximately 51 genes associated with eight meiotic complexes across 27 species. The final number of genes, families, and species may change after sequence curation and validation.

## Objectives

The main objectives of the project are the following: 

* characterize protein conservation and evolutionary divergence
* reconstruct gene-family relationships
* evaluate patterns of synonymous and nonsynonymous evolution
* identify possible evidence of selection at the gene, site, and lineage levels
* compare evolutionary patterns among meiotic genes and complexes
